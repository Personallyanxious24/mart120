My Art
This is my painting! I just finished it this weekend. It speaks on my battle with depression. I've struggled with it for years now and sometimes I wonder how many I still have to go. However, this painting is more optomistic. In the strain I feel while being depressed is now battled off with effort I put in to be healthy. I find myself stressed knowing how much further I must go to be able to handle my depression fully. But this painting means a lot to me knowing how hard I work to be mentally stable.
